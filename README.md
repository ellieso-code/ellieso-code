## How I Work

- **Start small, but start now.** Don't wait for perfect. Get something in users' hands and learn from there.
- **Users are the guiding light.** Build for them and with them. If you delight even a small group, they become your champions.
- **MVP is a launchpad.** It's not about minimum work. It's about maximum learning.
- **AI-native.** AI is how I think, draft, and build. It's not a novelty, it's the workflow.
- **Never stop iterating.** Every feature is a test. Cut what doesn't work, double down on what does.
